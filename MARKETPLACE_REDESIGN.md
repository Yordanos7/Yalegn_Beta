## Design System

### Colors

- **Primary**: Yellow (#EAB308) for CTAs and accents
- **Success**: Green for new items
- **Info**: Blue for ratings
- **Theme-aware**: All backgrounds and text adapt to light/dark mode

### Spacing

- Consistent padding and margins
- Proper card spacing (gap-6)
- Responsive grid (1-4 columns based on screen size)

### Typography

- Bold headings with gradient effects
- Clear hierarchy
- Readable font sizes
